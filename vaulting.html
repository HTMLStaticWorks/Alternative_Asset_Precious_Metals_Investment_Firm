Security Infrastructure

World-Class Vaulting

Institutional-grade security across four global jurisdictions.

Allocated Storage

Your metals are stored separately from all other holdings — never pooled or commingled. Each bar and coin is individually identified and assigned to your account.

Full Insurance

Every ounce is insured by Lloyd's of London against theft, damage, and natural disaster. Coverage equals the full replacement value of your holdings at market price.

Independent Audits

Quarterly physical audits by Deloitte verify that every bar and coin matches our records. Audit certificates are published to your client dashboard within 48 hours.

Multi-Jurisdiction

Store assets across Zurich, London, Singapore, and New York. Diversify sovereign risk with seamless cross-border transfers at no additional cost.

Biometric Access

Physical vault access requires multi-factor biometric authentication. Remote client verification uses encrypted two-factor protocols for all transactions.

24/7 Surveillance

Continuous CCTV monitoring with AI-powered anomaly detection, armed guard patrols, and seismic sensors across all vault locations.
