Our Heritage

About Aurex Capital

Protecting and growing wealth through precious metals since the 2008 financial crisis.

Our Story

Born From Conviction

When the 2008 financial crisis exposed the fragility of traditional financial systems, our founders — a former central bank advisor and a commodity trading veteran — saw an opportunity to build something different.

They envisioned a firm that would give individual investors the same institutional-grade access to precious metals that sovereign wealth funds and central banks had long enjoyed. Aurex Capital was born from the belief that every investor deserves transparent, secure, and affordable access to the world's most enduring assets.

Today, we manage over $4.2 billion in allocated precious metals for more than 18,000 clients across 42 countries.

Our Mission

To democratize access to precious metals investing by providing institutional-grade storage, transparent pricing, and expert guidance to investors of all sizes.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted platform for alternative asset investment, where every ounce is allocated, every transaction is transparent, and every client is valued.

Milestones

Our Journey

2008

Founded

Aurex Capital established in Zurich by James Hartwell and Dr. Lena Fischer during the global financial crisis.

2011

LBMA Accreditation

Became an LBMA-approved market participant, enabling direct access to London fixing prices for all clients.

2014

Singapore Vault Launch

Expanded into Asia with a state-of-the-art vault facility in Singapore's Freeport district.

2018

$1 Billion AUM

Crossed $1 billion in assets under management, serving over 5,000 clients across 28 countries.

2021

Digital Platform Launch

Launched our client portal with real-time portfolio tracking, instant trading, and digital audit certificates.

2024

$4.2 Billion AUM

Now serving 18,000+ clients across 42 countries with four global vault locations and growing.
