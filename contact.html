FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you need? Send us a message above.

What is the minimum investment?

You can start with as little as $5,000. Larger allocations of $100,000 or more are handled by our institutional desk with custom pricing.

Do I actually own the metal?

Yes. All holdings are fully allocated, which means specific bars and coins are recorded in your name and stored separately from other clients' metal. You receive a vault certificate every quarter.

Where is my metal stored, and is it insured?

Your metal is held in LBMA-approved vaults in Zurich, London, Singapore, or New York, the location is your choice. Every holding is fully insured by Lloyd's of London and independently audited.

What fees do you charge?

There is a small spread between the buy and sell price, plus an annual storage and insurance fee based on the value you hold. The full cost is shown before you confirm any order. There are no hidden charges.

How quickly can I sell?

You can sell back to us on any business day at the live market price. Proceeds are usually paid to your bank account within two business days.

Can I take delivery of my bars and coins?

Yes. You can request insured, fully tracked delivery of your metal to an address of your choice. Delivery costs depend on the destination and the value shipped.

Can I hold precious metals in a retirement account?

Yes. We offer Precious Metals IRAs for eligible gold, silver, platinum, and palladium, and can help you roll over an existing account. We recommend speaking to a tax advisor first.

How fast will you reply to my message?

Our client team replies within one business day, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM CET. Existing clients can also reach their account manager directly from the dashboard.
