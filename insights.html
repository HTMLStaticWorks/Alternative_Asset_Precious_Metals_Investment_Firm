Gold Breaks Through $2,300 Amid Central Bank Buying

Central bank purchases reached a record 1,136 tonnes in 2023, and the trend shows no signs of slowing. China, Poland, and Turkey led the buying, signaling a structural shift away from dollar-denominated reserves.

Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve's pivot toward rate cuts has further weakened the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets, creating a tailwind for gold and silver prices through 2024.
